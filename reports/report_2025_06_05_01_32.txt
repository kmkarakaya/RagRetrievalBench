=== MAP Calculation Methodology ===
The evaluator's MAP calculation is a simple macro-average of per-query AP values.
For each query, Average Precision (AP) is calculated as:
  AP = (sum of precision@i for each relevant doc i found) / min(k, total number of relevant docs)

Then MAP is calculated as:
  MAP = sum(AP_i) / number_of_queries
This is different from the traditional weighted MAP calculation that weights by relevant doc count.

=== Note on Rankings and Metrics ===
All metrics in this report (both summary and per-query) use the SentenceTransformer evaluator's
internal tensor-based cosine similarity for document ranking. This ensures consistency between
the summary metrics and per-query metrics.

Note: The evaluator's ranking may differ from ChromaDB's ranking in production environments.

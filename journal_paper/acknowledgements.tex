\section*{Acknowledgements}

An AI-assisted tool was used in preparing this paper and its accompanying software. Claude
Code (Anthropic; \url{https://claude.com/claude-code}; version 2.1, with the Claude Opus 5
and Claude Opus 5.5 models) was used for language editing, LaTeX formatting against the journal template, and assistance with
the pipeline's build and reporting scripts. The author designed the study and the pipeline,
reviewed and edited all AI-assisted text and code, and takes full responsibility for the
content of the paper. To keep the released software checkable independently of how it was
written, the repository includes an automated test suite and a consistency check
(\texttt{-{}-verify-paper}) that compares the numbers quoted in the paper with the stored run
artifacts. No AI tool was
used to produce or interpret the reported results; the research use of Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite
as a query generator and relevance judge is described in
Section~\ref{shared-evaluation-setup}.

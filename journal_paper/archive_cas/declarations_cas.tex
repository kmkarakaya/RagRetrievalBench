\section*{Code and data availability}

The pipeline implementation and the benchmark artifacts reported in this paper are available
in the project repository \citep{GitHub_RagRetrievalBench}. The released artifacts comprise
the source documents, chunked corpora, generated synthetic queries, LLM-judged qrels
including explicit negative labels, per-model ranking files, raw-result JSON, and the
generated dataset-level and aggregate reports for all three corpora. Persisted ChromaDB
vector indexes are excluded from the release and can be rebuilt from the chunked corpora with
the pipeline's indexing stage. The source documents are public institutional regulations
obtained from the providers listed in Table~\ref{tbl:dataset_provenance}
\citep{TED_Regulations, BDDK_Mevzuat}; the reproducibility conditions under which the reported
snapshot was produced are summarized in Table~\ref{tbl:reproducibility}.
